Frequently Asked Questions

What is a disposable email?
A disposable email is a temporary email address that self-destructs after a set time. Use it for signups and verifications without exposing your real inbox to spam.
Is vitsmail secure?
Yes. All incoming emails are sanitized to remove scripts and tracking pixels. We never store your IP, and mailboxes are permanently deleted after 30 minutes.
How long does the email last?
Each mailbox automatically expires after 30 minutes. You'll see a live countdown. When it hits zero, all data is permanently deleted from our servers.
Can I send emails from a vitsmail address?
No. vitsmail is receive-only. This prevents spam abuse and keeps the service focused on privacy protection.
Is it really free?
Yes, completely free. No hidden tiers, no premium plans, no paid features. Generate unlimited temporary emails.
